Technical Support

Help with Image Toolkit on iPhone. Requires iOS 15 or later.

Common questions

Why can’t I import a photo?

Choose a JPEG, PNG, or HEIC photo. If it is stored in iCloud or another provider, open or download the original first and try again.

Why can’t an image reach my file size limit?

A low limit may require smaller dimensions or a different format. Try JPG for photos, increase the limit, or allow smaller pixel dimensions. Check the actual output size on the Results screen.

How do I save or share the result?

Use Save to Photos, Save to Files, or Share on the Results screen. If Photos permission is unavailable, enable it in iOS Settings or save a copy to Files. PDFs can be saved to Files or shared.

How do I restore Image Toolkit Pro?

Open Settings in Image Toolkit and tap Restore Purchase. Use the same Apple Account you used to buy Pro. Purchases and refund requests are handled by Apple.

What is included for free?

Single-photo tools, the first three photos per batch, PDFs of up to five pages, and collages of up to nine photos. You can edit and preview watermarks for free, then export without the watermark or unlock watermark export with Pro.
