Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

Photos and files

Image Toolkit processes the photos, files, and watermark images you select on your device. The app does not upload them to the developer. It uses the system photo picker and requests permission to add photos only when you choose to save to Photos. If an original is stored with iCloud or another file provider, that provider may need to download it to your device before the app can open it.

Temporary files and settings

The app uses temporary working copies to create and preview exports. It normally removes these working copies when you leave the tool and checks for old temporary files on launch. Last-used settings and named presets are stored locally. A named preset can contain watermark text you choose to save. You can delete named presets in the app. To remove the app’s local data, delete the app from your device. Copies saved to Photos, Files, or another app are managed separately.

Location and sharing

Single-photo tools remove embedded location information by default. You can turn off Remove Location when you want to retain it. Export creates a new file and does not overwrite your original. If you share an export or save it to a cloud provider, the service you choose handles it according to its own privacy practices.

Purchases

Apple handles Pro purchases and payment information. The app uses Apple StoreKit to check and restore Pro access. The developer does not receive your payment card details. Apple may process information through its App Store services under its own privacy policy.

Accounts, tracking and advertising

The app does not require a developer account, include advertising or analytics SDKs, or track you across apps and websites. These support pages use no analytics scripts, advertising, or site-set cookies. The hosting provider may process standard connection information to deliver and secure the pages.

Support and privacy questions

When you contact support, the information you choose to send, such as your reply address and a description of an issue, is used to respond to and troubleshoot your request. You can use the same contact to ask about access to or deletion of your correspondence. Please avoid sending private photos or documents.

For questions or requests, visit Technical Support.
